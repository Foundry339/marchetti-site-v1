Full Bio

From the classroom to the keynote stage

Steve Marchetti began his career as a middle school teacher before moving into school and district leadership, where he spent years navigating technology adoption, teacher training, and change management at scale.

When generative AI entered the classroom conversation, Steve saw the same pattern he had seen before: well-meaning policy written faster than practical guidance. He now works full-time helping schools close that gap, through keynotes, workshops, and ongoing consulting with district leadership teams.

His talks are known for being refreshingly free of hype: grounded in real classroom examples, honest about the risks, and always focused on what a teacher can do differently by the following week.
