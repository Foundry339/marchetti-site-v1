AI literacy is quickly becoming as foundational as reading and math, yet most districts still treat it like an elective bolted onto the computer lab.

## The problem with siloed AI instruction

When only one teacher in a building understands generative AI, students get an inconsistent, disconnected picture of the tools that are already shaping their world outside of school.

## What a literate classroom actually looks like

- Teachers model responsible use openly, instead of banning tools and hoping students comply
- Students learn to question AI output the same way they learn to question a source
- Assessment design assumes AI exists, rather than pretending it does not

> The goal is not to make every teacher a computer science teacher. The goal is to make every teacher comfortable enough to have an honest conversation about the tools students already carry in their pockets.

That shift starts with permission from leadership, and it starts with modeling from the front of the room.
