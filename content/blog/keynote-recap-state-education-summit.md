I spent three days last week talking with nearly 900 educators about AI in the classroom, and the same handful of questions kept surfacing.

## "Are we moving too fast?"

My honest answer: the technology is moving fast. Our pedagogy does not have to sprint to keep up, it just has to stay in the conversation.

## "What about academic integrity?"

This is less an AI problem and more a mirror held up to assessment design that was already fragile before generative tools existed.

## "Where do we even start?"

Start small. Pick one unit, one assignment, one honest conversation with students about expectations. Momentum builds from there.
